Taste of Sonada

Taste of Sonada is the highest-rated cafe in Sonada, Darjeeling. We serve fresh breakfast, burgers, and authentic Darjeeling tea daily.

What is Taste of Sonada?

Taste of Sonada is a premium cafe located in Chaikhola near Green Hill School. We opened to serve locals and tourists. We serve over 100 customers daily. 95% of our reviews are 5-star ratings. Our average price is ₹300 per person.

How to reach Taste of Sonada?

Reaching us is very simple. Take the Kurseong-Darjeeling highway. Stop at Chaikhola in Sonada. Walk towards Green Hill School. Look for our cafe.

Why choose Taste of Sonada?

  • We use 100% locally sourced organic Darjeeling tea.
  • Our burgers are made with freshly baked artisan buns.
  • We offer cozy hillside seating with a view.
